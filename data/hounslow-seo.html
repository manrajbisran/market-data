Current property prices in Hounslow

The average property price in Hounslow was £513,853 in September 2026, according to the UK House Price Index, down 3.6% on a year earlier. Private rents averaged £1,970 a month in October 2026, up 4.0% on a year earlier. HM Land Registry has registered 1,692 sales in Hounslow in the 12 months to August 2026.

Best areas to buy property in Hounslow

Over the 12 months to August 2026, W4 (Chiswick) had the highest median sale price of Hounslow's postcode districts at £820,000, and TW13 (Feltham, Hanworth) the lowest at £428,725.

Hounslow lettings market

The average monthly rent in Hounslow is £1,970: £1,593 for one bedroom, £1,963 for two bedrooms, £2,265 for three bedrooms and £3,106 for four or more. Among Hounslow and its neighbouring boroughs, rents are highest in Richmond upon Thames (£2,367) and lowest in Hillingdon (£1,594).
