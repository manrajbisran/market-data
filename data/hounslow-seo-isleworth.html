Isleworth property prices

The median price paid for a home in Isleworth was £490,000 in the 12 months to July 2026, based on 101 sales registered with HM Land Registry, down 1.8% on the previous 12 months.

How Isleworth compares

That makes Isleworth the fourth most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £420,000 in Feltham to £610,000 in Whitton.

Isleworth prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Isleworth were £588,500 for semi-detached homes (14 sales), £642,526 for terraced homes (43 sales) and £318,750 for flats and maisonettes (44 sales).

1 Year house price change in Isleworth

Renting in Isleworth

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Isleworth: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026, up 3.6% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £528,051, that is a gross yield of about 4.5% before costs.
