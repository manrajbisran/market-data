Isleworth property prices

The median price paid for a home in Isleworth was £525,000 in the 12 months to August 2026, based on 101 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 9.9% on the previous 12 months.

How Isleworth compares

That makes Isleworth the second most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £425,000 in Feltham to £598,500 in Whitton.

Isleworth prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Isleworth were £588,500 for semi-detached homes (18 sales), £660,000 for terraced homes (45 sales) and £330,000 for flats and maisonettes (38 sales).

3 Month house price change in Isleworth

Over the past 3 months, the median price paid in Isleworth rose 10.2%. Across our 8 areas, Isleworth saw the strongest change (+10.2%) and Heston the weakest (-2.9%).

1 Year house price change in Isleworth

Over the past year, the median price paid in Isleworth rose 9.9%. Across our 8 areas, Cranford saw the strongest change (+13.3%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

3 Year house price change in Isleworth

Over the past 3 years, the median price paid in Isleworth fell 0.9%. Across our 8 areas, Osterley saw the strongest change (+14.9%) and Brentford the weakest (-13.8%).

5 Year house price change in Isleworth

Over the past 5 years, the median price paid in Isleworth rose 4.7%. Across our 8 areas, Cranford saw the strongest change (+48.3%) and Brentford the weakest (-1.1%).

Renting in Isleworth

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Isleworth: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,970 a month in October 2026, up 3.9% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £513,853, that is a gross yield of about 4.6% before costs.
