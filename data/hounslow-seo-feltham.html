Feltham property prices

The median price paid for a home in Feltham was £420,000 in the 12 months to July 2026, based on 173 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 3.7% on the previous 12 months.

How Feltham compares

That makes Feltham the least expensive of the 8 areas we cover; the next is Brentford at £432,500.

Feltham prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Feltham were £485,000 for semi-detached homes (49 sales), £439,500 for terraced homes (62 sales) and £250,000 for flats and maisonettes (58 sales).
