Feltham property prices

The median price paid for a home in Feltham was £425,000 in the 12 months to August 2026, based on 175 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 4.9% on the previous 12 months.

How Feltham compares

That makes Feltham the least expensive of the 8 areas we cover; the next is Brentford at £445,000.

Feltham prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Feltham were £485,000 for semi-detached homes (54 sales), £440,000 for terraced homes (63 sales) and £250,000 for flats and maisonettes (54 sales).

3 Month house price change in Feltham

Over the past 3 months, the median price paid in Feltham rose 1.2%. Across our 8 areas, Isleworth saw the strongest change (+10.2%) and Heston the weakest (-2.9%).

1 Year house price change in Feltham

Over the past year, the median price paid in Feltham rose 4.9%. Across our 8 areas, Cranford saw the strongest change (+13.3%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

3 Year house price change in Feltham

5 Year house price change in Feltham

Renting in Feltham

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Feltham: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026, up 3.6% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £528,051, that is a gross yield of about 4.5% before costs.
