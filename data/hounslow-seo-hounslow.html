Hounslow property prices

The median price paid for a home in Hounslow was £450,000 in the 12 months to July 2026, based on 287 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 1.5% on the previous 12 months.

How Hounslow compares

That makes Hounslow the sixth most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £420,000 in Feltham to £610,000 in Whitton.

Hounslow prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Hounslow were £525,000 for detached homes (7 sales), £572,500 for semi-detached homes (93 sales), £484,000 for terraced homes (84 sales) and £290,000 for flats and maisonettes (103 sales).

Renting in Hounslow

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Hounslow: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026, up 3.6% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £528,051, that is a gross yield of about 4.5% before costs.
