Cranford property prices

The median price paid for a home in Cranford was £510,000 in the 12 months to August 2026, based on 35 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 13.3% on the previous 12 months. Cranford is a small area with relatively few sales, so its figures can move sharply from one period to the next.

How Cranford compares

That makes Cranford the third most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £425,000 in Feltham to £598,500 in Whitton.

Cranford prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Cranford were £627,500 for semi-detached homes (8 sales), £535,000 for terraced homes (17 sales) and £281,000 for flats and maisonettes (10 sales).

3 Month house price change in Cranford

Over the past 3 months, the median price paid in Cranford rose 3.0%. Across our 8 areas, Isleworth saw the strongest change (+10.2%) and Heston the weakest (-2.9%).

1 Year house price change in Cranford

Over the past year, the median price paid in Cranford rose 13.3%. Across our 8 areas, Cranford saw the strongest change (+13.3%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

3 Year house price change in Cranford

Over the past 3 years, the median price paid in Cranford rose 5.4%. Across our 8 areas, Osterley saw the strongest change (+14.9%) and Brentford the weakest (-13.8%).

5 Year house price change in Cranford

Renting in Cranford

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Cranford: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026, up 3.6% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £528,051, that is a gross yield of about 4.5% before costs.
