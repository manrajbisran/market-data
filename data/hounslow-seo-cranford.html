Cranford property prices

The median price paid for a home in Cranford was £470,000 in the 12 months to July 2026, based on 36 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 4.4% on the previous 12 months. Cranford is a small area with relatively few sales, so its figures can move sharply from one period to the next.

How Cranford compares

That makes Cranford the fifth most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £420,000 in Feltham to £610,000 in Whitton.

Cranford prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Cranford were £565,000 for semi-detached homes (9 sales), £527,500 for terraced homes (16 sales) and £255,000 for flats and maisonettes (11 sales).

Renting in Cranford

Rents are published for the whole borough rather than for Cranford: private rents in Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026, up 3.6% on a year earlier. Set against the Hounslow average price of £528,051, that is a gross yield of about 4.5% before costs.
