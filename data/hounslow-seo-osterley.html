Osterley property prices

The median price paid for a home in Osterley was £500,000 in the 12 months to August 2026, based on 101 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 2.5% on the previous 12 months.

How Osterley compares

That makes Osterley the fifth most expensive of the 8 areas we cover, where medians range from £425,000 in Feltham to £598,500 in Whitton.

Osterley prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Osterley were £771,000 for semi-detached homes (30 sales), £650,000 for terraced homes (23 sales) and £337,500 for flats and maisonettes (44 sales).

3 Month house price change in Osterley

Over the past 3 months, the median price paid in Osterley rose 1.2%. Across our 8 areas, Isleworth saw the strongest change (+10.2%) and Heston the weakest (-2.9%).

1 Year house price change in Osterley

Renting in Osterley
