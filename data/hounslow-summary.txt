The average property price in Hounslow was £528,051 in August 2026, according to the UK House Price Index, down 1.5% on a year earlier. By property type, the average was £1,047,015 for detached homes, £720,301 for semi-detached homes, £617,676 for terraced homes and £353,291 for flats and maisonettes. HM Land Registry has registered 1,692 sales in the borough in the 12 months to August 2026, with a median price of £480,000 (down 2.0% on the previous 12 months). Median sale prices over the same period were £440,000 in TW3 (Hounslow) from 199 sales, £460,000 in TW4 (Hounslow West, Cranford) from 105 sales, £510,000 in TW5 (Heston) from 97 sales and £500,000 in TW7 (Isleworth, Osterley) from 304 sales. Figures are updated monthly from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data and the UK House Price Index. Recent months may be revised as late sales are registered.
