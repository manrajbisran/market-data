Property prices across our areas

Across the 8 areas we cover, HM Land Registry has registered 1,226 home sales in the 12 months to July 2026. Median prices range from £420,000 in Feltham to £610,000 in Whitton.

Prices by property type

By property type, detached homes cost most in Osterley (£775,000) and least in Hounslow (£525,000); semi-detached homes cost most in Osterley (£757,000) and least in Feltham (£485,000); terraced homes cost most in Brentford (£662,500) and least in Feltham (£439,500); flats and maisonettes cost most in Brentford (£362,500) and least in Feltham (£250,000).

3 Month house price change across our areas

Over the past 3 months, median prices rose in 3 of the 8 areas. Hounslow saw the strongest change (+5.9%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

1 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past year, median prices rose in 5 of the 8 areas. Osterley saw the strongest change (+7.1%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

3 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past 3 years, median prices rose in 4 of the 8 areas. Osterley saw the strongest change (+14.8%) and Brentford the weakest (-14.4%).

5 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past 5 years, median prices rose in 6 of the 8 areas. Cranford saw the strongest change (+38.2%) and Brentford the weakest (-3.9%).

Rents across our areas

Rents are published by borough: Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026 (a gross yield of about 4.5%) and Richmond upon Thames averaged £2,349 a month in September 2026 (a gross yield of about 3.5%).
