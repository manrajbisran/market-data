Property prices across our areas

Across the 8 areas we cover, HM Land Registry has registered 1,249 home sales in the 12 months to August 2026. Median prices range from £425,000 in Feltham to £598,500 in Whitton.

Prices by property type

By property type, detached homes cost most in Heston (£787,500) and least in Hounslow (£592,160); semi-detached homes cost most in Osterley (£771,000) and least in Feltham (£485,000); terraced homes cost most in Brentford (£682,500) and least in Feltham (£440,000); flats and maisonettes cost most in Brentford (£360,000) and least in Feltham (£250,000).

3 Month house price change across our areas

Over the past 3 months, median prices rose in 6 of the 8 areas. Isleworth saw the strongest change (+10.2%) and Heston the weakest (-2.9%).

1 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past year, median prices rose in 5 of the 8 areas. Cranford saw the strongest change (+13.3%) and Heston the weakest (-4.3%).

3 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past 3 years, median prices rose in 5 of the 8 areas. Osterley saw the strongest change (+14.9%) and Brentford the weakest (-13.8%).

5 Year house price change across our areas

Over the past 5 years, median prices rose in 7 of the 8 areas. Cranford saw the strongest change (+48.3%) and Brentford the weakest (-1.1%).

Rents across our areas

Rents are published by borough: Hounslow averaged £1,962 a month in September 2026 (a gross yield of about 4.5%) and Richmond upon Thames averaged £2,349 a month in September 2026 (a gross yield of about 3.5%).
