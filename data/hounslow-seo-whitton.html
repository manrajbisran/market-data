Whitton property prices

The median price paid for a home in Whitton was £598,500 in the 12 months to August 2026, based on 170 sales registered with HM Land Registry, down 0.2% on the previous 12 months.

How Whitton compares

That makes Whitton the most expensive of the 8 areas we cover; the next is Isleworth at £525,000.

Whitton prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Whitton were £685,000 for detached homes (11 sales), £666,250 for semi-detached homes (84 sales), £576,250 for terraced homes (42 sales) and £350,000 for flats and maisonettes (33 sales).
