Whitton property prices

The median price paid for a home in Whitton was £610,000 in the 12 months to July 2026, based on 173 sales registered with HM Land Registry, up 1.8% on the previous 12 months.

How Whitton compares

That makes Whitton the most expensive of the 8 areas we cover; the next is Osterley at £525,000.

Whitton prices by property type

By property type, median prices in Whitton were £690,000 for detached homes (12 sales), £680,000 for semi-detached homes (85 sales), £576,250 for terraced homes (46 sales) and £360,000 for flats and maisonettes (30 sales).
